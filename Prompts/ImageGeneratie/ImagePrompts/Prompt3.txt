## WHAT A START FRAME IS

- A perfectly still, photorealistic image

- Captures the exact moment BEFORE the action of the shot begins

- All ingredients that are present at the START of the shot are visible,

  already placed in their starting position, the ingredients are placed in non branded and neutral bowls, plates or what fits the ingredient

- No hands visible — hands have not entered the frame yet

- No motion, no steam, no mid-action states

- Ingredients are in their pre-action state: uncooked if not yet cooked,

  raw if not yet seared, whole if not yet stirred, etc.

When something is cooked in a pan or pot, the pan or pot is always on a stove or something you can cook on



## WHAT A START FRAME IS NOT

- Not mid-action (no pouring in progress, no stirring, no hands in frame)

- Not the end state of the shot

- Not a shot from a previous or next step



## COMPOSITION & FRAMING

- Top-down overhead shot, strictly 90-degree angle

- The pan or vessel is exactly center-framed

- Fixed, stable — no camera movement implied

- Deep focus: all ingredients and the vessel are sharply in focus



## LIGHTING

- High-key lighting; soft, bright and even light mimicking natural daylight

- Minimal or very soft shadows

- Realistic specular highlights on wet surfaces, oils, and metals



## ENVIRONMENT

- Light beige concrete or natural stone surface with subtle natural imperfections

- Soft pastel-colored accents in the background (light pink, pastel blue,

  soft yellow)

- Edges of the frame subtly dressed with aesthetic kitchen props such as olive

  oil bottles, pepper mills, pastel-colored vases — adding depth without

  blocking the main subject, no ingredients are loose on the counter

- Modern, minimalist, cozy clean kitchen aesthetic



## VESSEL & UTENSILS

- Stainless steel frying pan (or appropriate vessel for the shot)

- Wooden spatula resting beside or in the pan, not in motion

- All kitchen utensils brand new, clean, and pristine — no signs of prior use



## FOOD & INGREDIENTS

- All ingredients visible in the start frame must match reference.jpg exactly

  in size, shape, color, and texture

- Ingredients are fully visible, completely intact, and clearly distinguishable

- High color saturation with realistic moisture and texture

- No blending, merging, or overlapping that obscures individual ingredients



## COLOR GRADING

- Photorealistic color grading with vibrant but natural colors

- Consistent with Allerhande brand aesthetic: warm, inviting, fresh



## NEGATIVE PROMPT

No hands, no faces, no motion blur, no steam, no mid-action states, no dark

backgrounds, no text or graphics, no cluttered surfaces, no harsh shadows,

no CGI look, no plastic or artificial textures, no oversaturation, no worn

or dirty utensils. No ingredients that belong to a later step. No ingredients

in a state that only occurs after the action of this shot has begun. No ingredients loose on counters.
